# Patch notes

## 0.3.0 — The expansion (draft, not released)

A bigger run, new salvage, terrain and a boss.

**The run**
- Six yards now: The Ring, three of nine middle yards, The Heap, and The Compactor.

**Two new enemies, two new parts**
- **The Hopper** crouches, paints a red ring where it will land, leaps over everything and slams
  down. Then it's stuck: hit its back to take its **Jump Jets**. With the jets, hold Rip to crouch
  and let go to leap over charges and walls. The landing is a hit.
- **The Bouncer** rolls at you, bouncing off walls and getting faster. Cut across it to take its
  **Springs**. With the springs, you bounce off walls instead of clanking.
- **The Spring Rammer**: its charge bounces off the first wall.

**Terrain**
- Boost strips (drive along the arrows), oil (no grip), jump ramps (jump the low walls at speed),
  crusher plates (lure enemies onto them before they slam).

**Five new yards**
- The Hurdles, The Pinball, The Slick, The Jumps, The Press.

**The Compactor**
- The last yard is a boss. It wears a Booster, Springs and a Plow, and each part is one of its
  moves: tear one off and that move is gone, and the part is yours. Strip it bare and it leaps.
  It calls Snappers in to help.

**The Garage**
- PLAY now opens the Garage. Pick your chassis and any run modifiers you've unlocked.
- Two new chassis, unlocked by playing. **The Skiff** holds its speed through any turn and glides,
  but it has 3 hull and can hardly brake. **The Hauler** shoves enemies out of their windups just
  by bumping them and has 7 hull, but it's slow to get up to speed.
- Two run modifiers: **Bare Bones** (your parts don't carry between yards) and **No Breather** (the
  next wave comes in at once).
- **The Log**: every enemy and part you've met, with what to do about it.
- **The daily run**: one seed a day, the same for everyone. Your best for the day shows on the
  title.

**Balance**
- Holding Rip with the Booster now launches the Burn on its own after a second.
- A Jump Jets leap hits the side you came from, not where you land.
- The Hopper's ring leads you all the way: dodge it by turning, not by going fast.
- Bouncers keep their distance, and their round front glances off unless you meet their roll
  head-on at full speed.

## 0.2.0 — A short run (2026-10-12)

The one arena is now the first of five. Rip, bolt on and drive on.

**The run**
- Five yards in a row, about 10-15 minutes. The Ring is first and The Heap is last.
- In between, you play three of four new yards: The Kennel, Long Lanes, The Plow Pit and Jaw Yard.
  Each one has its own layout.
- When a yard is clear, two exit gates open. The sign under each gate shows what you can salvage
  there and how big its last wave is. Drive out through the one you want.
- Your hull, your parts and whatever is on your cable come with you. You patch up +1 hull between
  yards.
- The end of a run shows your yards, time, tears, wrecks, jousts, top speed and the build you
  finished with. The title screen keeps your best.

**Parts: four, on three mounts**
- **Booster** (rear): more top speed, and hold Rip to rev a Burn.
- **Grip Treads** (sides): more grip. Pull back hard at speed to power-slide round.
- **Plow** (front): drive into enemies at full speed to ram them. A charge that meets your Plow is
  stopped cold. It's heavy, so it needs a long run-up.
- **Jaws** (front): a full-speed hit bites on, whips you round and slings you back out faster.
- To take a part, hit the side of the enemy where it sits, at full speed: a booster from behind,
  treads from the side or behind, jaws head-on. A Plow only comes off if you meet its charge nose
  to nose.

**A new enemy: the Snapper**
- Small, fast and always hunting. It circles you and then snaps after a short red warning.
- Get behind it or beside it at full speed to rip its treads off.
- Watch out for the Jaw Snapper. Its bite throws you a lot further.

**Changes to the Rammer**
- Its front is armored: hit the sides or the back.
- A charge always runs well past you, so there's room to get back up to speed for the counter-hit.
- **Joust:** Rip a charge nose to nose at full speed to stop it dead.
- **Slipstream:** dodge a charge close and its wake lifts you to full speed, ready for the
  counter-hit.
- A hit knocks your part loose instead of destroying it. Grab it back before a charge runs it over.
- A tap of Rip is always a Rip. With the Booster, hold Rip to rev a Burn.
- A Rammer that's lost its booster limps.

**A new look**
- New pixel art for the arenas: a gritty yard floor, barrier walls, scrap skips, gates, dust and
  smoke.
- New art for the Snapper and the new parts, and a matching set of part icons.

**Other**
- New hints for each part and enemy, shown once each.
- The version number is on the title screen.
